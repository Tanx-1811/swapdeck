# Code of conduct

Swapdeck follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and never harass,
insult or share anyone's private information. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you
represent the project.

**Reporting:** if someone's behaviour is unacceptable, tell the maintainer privately — use the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form and start the title with "Conduct report", or contact
[@Tanx-1811](https://github.com/Tanx-1811) on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or
reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code.

---

**Tiếng Việt:** Swapdeck áp dụng [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Hãy tôn trọng nhau, góp ý xây dựng, không quấy rối, không xúc phạm, không chia sẻ thông tin riêng tư của người khác.
Báo cáo vi phạm một cách riêng tư qua **Security → Report a vulnerability** (tiêu đề bắt đầu bằng "Conduct report")
hoặc liên hệ [@Tanx-1811](https://github.com/Tanx-1811).
